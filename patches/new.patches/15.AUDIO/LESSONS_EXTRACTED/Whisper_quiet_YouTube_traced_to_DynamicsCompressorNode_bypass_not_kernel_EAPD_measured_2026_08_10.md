# Whisper_quiet_YouTube_traced_to_DynamicsCompressorNode_bypass_not_kernel_EAPD_measured_2026_08_10

**Source:** Whisper_quiet_YouTube_traced_to_DynamicsCompressorNode_bypass_not_kernel_EAPD_measured_2026_08_10.xml

## Rationale

The laptop sounded whisper-quiet even with every volume slider past 100%. Everyone suspected the kernel speaker-amp patch. Measurement proved the speakers and kernel are fine: at full volume the speakers are loud enough to clip the internal microphone. The real thief was inside the Firefox fork: a patch that turns off the Web Audio 'compressor'. YouTube's Stable Volume feature quietly routes all sound through that compressor and counts on it to boost the signal back up (make-up gain). With the compressor bypassed, YouTube's audio arrives about 23 dB too quiet - a whisper - and no slider can recover it.

PROBLEM: All volume sliders pushed past 100% (pavucontrol 104-107%) yielded whisper-level sound on the Sony VAIO SVE14A3AJ under the 2026-08-10 kernel 7.1.2-unleashed build, while every mixer level (Master, Speaker, PCM, EAPD) looked healthy.

## Execution Logic

SOLUTION: Fix: remove the GORILLA bypass block in dom/media/webaudio/DynamicsCompressorNode.cpp ProcessBlock() (around line 88) and './mach build binaries'. Instant no-rebuild workaround: turn OFF 'Stable volume' in the YouTube player settings gear (routes audio back through the plain HTMLMediaElement path, where the AudioStream DSP and volume_scale 2.0 apply), or temporarily flip media.gorilla.hardware_only_mode=false in about:config (mirror: always, takes effect live - but this also relaxes the hardware-only codec policy, so revert after testing). Diagnostic method worth reusing: pw-record the app's own PipeWire node and compare digital level against the sink monitor and against a mic recording - it splits browser-internal loss from system-mixer loss from hardware loss in minutes.

Evidence chain, all measured on 2026-08-10: (1) ALSA/PipeWire software chain accounted for only ~8 dB attenuation (sink 73% = -8.2 dB, Master -7 dB, PCM -0.2 dB) - cannot explain a whisper. (2) EAPD verified FUNCTIONAL by experiment: 1237 Hz tone through speakers recorded via internal mic at -17 dB RMS with EAPD on; setting EAPD=0 on NID 0x14 via hwdep verb dropped it to -64 dB (noise floor); restored to 0x2. The driver raises EAPD on its own - no boot-time firmware fix is needed. (3) Max capability test (sink 100%, 0 dBFS tone) clipped the internal mic (0 dB peak) - hardware can be loud. (4) Firefox node 91 raw digital output during YouTube music: -24.3 dB peaks / -37.6 dB RMS, identical at sink monitor - the loss is INSIDE Firefox, upstream of PipeWire/ALSA/kernel. (5) Root cause: dom/media/webaudio/DynamicsCompressorNode.cpp GORILLA bypass ('*aOutput = aInput; return;' when media.gorilla.hardware_only_mode, default true, mirror always). YouTube Stable Volume routes playback through a WebAudio DynamicsCompressorNode and depends on its automatic make-up gain (Blink/WebKit-derived, can exceed +20 dB); bypassing removes the make-up gain. The patch's stated rationale ('prevents re-compression of AudioStream DSP output') is architecturally false: WebAudio graphs output via AudioCallbackDriver directly to cubeb and NEVER pass through AudioStream::DataCallback, so the AudioStream psychoacoustic enhancer and media.volume_scale 2.0 also never apply on the WebAudio path. The two paths are parallel, not chained. ALSO ESTABLISHED: the kernel ALC269_FIXUP_VAIO_EAPD_5DB patch is acoustically a no-op placebo - it only rewrites amp-capability METADATA (AC_AMPCAP_OFFSET) on NID 0x14 (whose out-amp is mute-only, nsteps=0) and NID 0x21 (a Vendor Defined Widget on this codec, not an amp). KERNEL.BRAIN blueprint itself marks '+5.5 dB' as never measured, and docs/alc269.c.md says the patch was decided REMOVED in 7.1.2, yet the 2026-08-10 build re-applied it with offset changed 0x04 to 0x14, unexplained. The 'firmware-driven EAPD boot fix' described in alc269.c.md (patch= loader) was never installed anywhere (no /etc/modprobe.d snd config, no firmware file) and measurement shows it is unnecessary.
