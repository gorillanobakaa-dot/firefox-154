## 1. WebCodecs-only software VP8 gate (the big one)

Full record: `FIrefox.154.Hardware.decode.WEBRTC/WEBCODECS-VP8-GATE.DEVELOPER.md`
(+ .LAYMAN.md); chroma lesson `WebCodecs_Software_VP8_Gate_Four_Stacked_Blockers`.

Design in one line: `DecoderAgent` builds its own `PDMFactory`; that factory is
constructed with `Purpose::WebCodecs` and, under the **default-false** pref
`media.gorilla.webcodecs_software_vp8`, registers `AgnosticDecoderModule` at the
FRONT and exempts **VP8 only** from the hardware-only block. `<video>`/MSE/
WebRTC factories are byte-identical in behaviour. Shipped defaults probe clean.

**Four stacked blockers were fixed — DO NOT rediscover them** (each hid the
next; full detail in the lesson): (1) `CanDecode` leaked into the Layer-1
`DecoderTraits` gate; (2) `all.js` `media.webm.enabled=false` starved
WebCodecs track-info (`NS_ERROR_ILLEGAL_VALUE`); (3) RDD/FFVPX claimed VP8 then
refused in `Init()` (`EncodingError`); (4) the registration initially sat in
the `media.allow-audio-non-utility` block — `@IS_IOS@` = dead code on Linux.

Loopback proof on the real binary: camera frames (luma 37–86) → VP8 encode
(5 chunks) → VP8 decode (5 frames), with `canPlayType(webm)=NO` and
`MSE(webm)=false` in the SAME profile. Steering intact.

This machine's `~/.mozilla/ff154-main/user.js` gained THREE lines (all
commented in-file): `webcodecs_software_vp8=true`, `media.webm.enabled=true`
(required companion — arms rather than bypasses the DecoderTraits hard lock),
`dom.max_script_run_time=0` (§3).

## 3. The WhatsApp UI wedge & the ICE red herring

After ALL codec layers were green, WhatsApp still wedged (black self-preview,
dead hang-up button, later grayed-out call buttons). Cause found in terminal
output: **"Script terminated by timeout"** — WhatsApp's call JS exceeded even
the build's raised 30 s slow-script limit on the i7-3632QM; the killed
script's handlers never recover. `dom.max_script_run_time=0` set in user.js
(shipped 30 kept). Lesson: `Slow_Script_Watchdog_Wedged_WhatsApp_Call_UI`.

**"WebRTC: ICE failed" is a closed case.** MOZ_LOG (`mtransport:5`) on the
final binary shows WhatsApp's page-load probes fully healthy: ICE pairs to
Meta edges (157.240.x.x:3478) SUCCEEDED + nominated, DTLS handshake completed,
SRTP keys set, consent refreshing, 163 STUN responses / 0 timeouts, clean 5 s
probe lifecycle. The console errors came from pre-fix binaries tearing down.
`media.peerconnection.ice.default_address_only` stays EXONERATED — now against
full connect+DTLS evidence, not just gathering.

Diagnostic traps recorded: about:webrtc "Save Page" after the call closes
dumps empty scaffolding — capture DURING the call or use MOZ_LOG. UVC camera
is single-consumer (`fuser /dev/video0` before probing). Wayland: `xwininfo`
cannot see native windows and agent shells lack XAUTHORITY — probe headless
with `dump()` + unique `MOZ_APP_REMOTINGNAME` instead.
